Brownies

Choose your texture

Made with real dark chocolate rather than cocoa powder alone, which is why they stay dense a day later. Tell us which texture you want — it changes the bake, not just the description.

Pick a texture
  • Fudgy — dense, gooey centre, very little rise. Our most requested.
  • Chewy — a little more lift, a soft pull, slightly lighter.
  • Cakey — closer to a chocolate cake square; the one to pick for kids.

Good to know

Lead time
  • Standard orders need at least 24 hours.
  • Whole cakes of 1 kg and above: 48 hours.
  • Special requests may be possible with 12 hours — just ask.
Ordering & delivery
  • Pickup from Canal Road, Surat.
  • Delivery across Surat; the charge depends on distance and is confirmed with your quote.
  • Advance payment by UPI confirms your slot. Balance on delivery.
Add-ons
  • Hand-piped message on a cake: ₹100
  • Eggless version of a standard bake: +₹40
  • Custom colours or a theme design: quoted with your cake enquiry
A note on allergens
  • Everything is baked in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, egg, soy and nuts.
  • We cannot guarantee an allergen-free bake. Please tell us about allergies and we will advise honestly.

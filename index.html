How it works

Three steps, one day ahead.

There is no basket to fill and no account to make. You tell us what you would like, we confirm the details, then we bake it fresh for your date.

01

Choose from the menu, or describe your idea

Regular items have prices listed. For a custom cake, tell us the weight, flavour and the feel you are after.

02

Send it on WhatsApp or through the form

Both go straight to the kitchen. You will get a reply confirming availability, the final price and your pickup or delivery slot.

03

Collect it, or have it delivered

Pickup is from Canal Road. Delivery runs across Surat — the charge depends on distance and is confirmed with your quote.

Please plan around the lead time Standard orders need at least 24 hours’ notice. Special requests can sometimes be arranged with as little as 12 hours — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is possible.

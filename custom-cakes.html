How a custom cake works

You choose four things. We handle the rest.

Custom cakes are quoted individually, but they are not a mystery. The price is driven by size and design complexity — both of which you control.

01

Size

From 500 g for a small gathering up to 3 kg and beyond for a large one. Serving guidance is listed below so you do not over-order.

02

Flavour

Chocolate truffle, Belgian chocolate, red velvet, butterscotch, hazelnut, Biscoff, vanilla, blueberry, coffee mocha — or something of your own.

03

Shape & design

Round, square, heart or a sheet cake. Simple finishes are included; detailed piping, fondant work and sculpted shapes are quoted separately.

04

The details

Eggless, a written message, a colour palette, or a reference photo you liked. Send it across and we will tell you what is realistic.

Our story

A home kitchen,
not a factory.

Sweet Fern began the way most home bakeries do — one good cheesecake, made for family, that people kept asking to buy. It has stayed deliberately small ever since.

Why we bake to order, not in advance.

A shop with a display case has to guess how much to bake each morning. We do not. Every order is placed first and baked for a specific date, which means nothing is sitting around, nothing is discounted at the end of the day, and nothing reaches you older than it should be.

That approach has a cost: we need notice. It is the one thing we genuinely cannot work around, and it is why every page on this site mentions the 24-hour lead time.

What we do not compromise on.

Real chocolate rather than compound. Actual vanilla, cream cheese and butter. Fruit cooked down in the kitchen instead of squeezed from a bottle. Biscuits crushed to make the base rather than bought ready-made.

It makes the ingredients bill higher and the batch size smaller. We think the difference is obvious in the first bite, which is why we have kept it this way.

Where you fit in.

Because everything is made to order, an order is a small conversation rather than a checkout. You tell us what you need and when. We tell you honestly what is achievable, what it will cost and how to collect it. That is the whole process — no app, no account, and no card details on this website.

How we bake

From your message to your table.

Four steps, and you are only involved in the first one and the last one.

STEP 01

You enquire

Send your date and what you have in mind, on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form. We reply with availability and a price.

STEP 02

We source

Ingredients are bought for your order specifically — cream, chocolate and fruit, fresh rather than from stock.

STEP 03

We bake

The bake starts the day before your slot. Cheesecakes are chilled overnight; cookies are baked as close to handover as possible.

STEP 04

You collect

Packed in kraft boxes so it travels well. Pickup from Canal Road, or we deliver anywhere in Surat.

What we stand for

The four things we will not trade away.

ONE

Small batches

Never more than the kitchen can do properly in a day. If we are full for a date, we will tell you rather than rush it.

TWO

Honest lead times

24 hours minimum, 48 for larger cakes. We would rather decline an order than send out something under-baked or under-chilled.

THREE

Prices up front

Our regular range has listed prices. You should not have to message a bakery just to find out what a box of cookies costs.

FOUR

Real ingredients

Chocolate, cream, butter and fruit — not premixes. It costs more and it tastes like it.

Once we have agreed the price and the date, a UPI advance confirms your slot. The balance is payable on delivery or at pickup. We never take payment through this website and we never ask for card details over chat.

Everything is baked in a single home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, egg, soy and nuts. That means we cannot guarantee an allergen-free bake, and we will not pretend otherwise. Brownies and brownie cakes: an airtight box at room temperature for 3–4 days, or refrigerate for up to a week. Cookies: airtight at room temperature for 4–5 days. Warm a brownie or cookie for 10 seconds before eating — it makes a real difference.
